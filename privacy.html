Privacy policy – rclone-ev

rclone-ev is used only by its owner, on the owner's own home server.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, to copy the owner's files there and read them back.
  • No data is collected, sold, or shared with anyone. Nothing is sent to any third party.
  • Google access tokens are stored only on the owner's server.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
